SPORT ANALYTICS · SYRACUSE UNIVERSITY

Hi, I’m Chris Antosiewicz.

I’m a cum laude Sport Analytics graduate from Syracuse who turns raw data into models and reports that decision-makers can use. I mostly work with football and baseball data, and I’ve worked with live data for an MLS academy, college athletic programs and Chicago’s economic development arm.

My senior thesis modeled 15 seasons of NFL play-by-play in R. I’m expanding on it with an automated weekly scouting report that uses SQL models to break down the trends behind every NFL team and show how your team can prepare for game day.

NFLCOMPLETED

Shotgun or Under Center: NFL Formation Recommender

When should an NFL offense line up in shotgun or under center? I modeled 15 seasons of nflfastR play-by-play in R and built a Shiny app that ranks formation and play calls for any situation. Under center converted 3rd-and-short more often in every season.

MLBIN PROGRESS

MLB Next-Pitch Model and Matchup Report

Predicting the next pitch type from game context on 2.85 million Statcast pitches. On a preregistered 2026 test, XGBoost hit 44.7% exact-pitch accuracy versus 37.2% for a pitcher’s usual mix. Includes a Shiny matchup app and an advance report.

NFLIN PROGRESS

NFL Game Forecasting Model

A Python model that predicts NFL moneyline, spread and total probabilities, tested walk-forward on 2019–2024 with preregistered rules. Adding kickoff rain and wind improved its totals; five quarterback feature sets did not help. It still trails the betting market.
